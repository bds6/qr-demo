About CLIve

CLIve has been trained on documentation for equipment available in selected Creative Learning Initiatives spaces. Ask about setup, controls, recommended settings, troubleshooting, or what to do when you finish.

The assistant uses the information encoded in the QR code to begin with the correct equipment. You can continue by typing follow-up questions in your own words.

  1. Review the first response. CLIve starts with a general introduction to the equipment identified by the QR code.
  2. Ask a specific question. Describe what you are trying to accomplish or what is not working. Short, direct questions work well.
  3. Request a consultant. If the assistant cannot resolve the issue, ask to speak with a Media Commons consultant. Availability depends on staffing.

Need more help?

Ask CLIve to connect you with a person, contact Media Commons directly, or schedule an appointment for more involved assistance.
